# Adrift — Game Overview

**Genre:** First-person survival / exploration / crafting.
**Engine:** Web — Babylon.js.
**Fantasy:** Stranded researcher turned wayfinder. Explore dead worlds to build a way home.

## Premise

A research vessel miscalculates a hyperdrive jump and grazes a moon. The ship breaks up. The player reaches an escape pod and emergency-lands on the planet the moon orbits — a world covered in ruins of a vanished civilization.

To survive and signal home, the player must:

1. Explore ruins and make discoveries.
2. Salvage, craft, and build a surface-to-orbit ship.
3. Hop between planets in the system.
4. Uncover what happened to the civilization.
5. Power a rescue beacon and attempt rescue.

## Pillars

- **Seamless worlds:** No loading screens between surface, orbit, and deep space.
- **Discovery as progression:** Knowledge unlocks survival and ship tech.
- **Lonely wonder:** Quiet ruins, alien skies, diegetic storytelling.
- **Plausible sci-fi:** Realistic light, gravity, and flight with arcade forgiveness.

## Tech Demo Goal

Prove seamless spaceflight and interplanetary travel in Babylon.js:

- Take off from a planet surface.
- Fly to orbit with atmospheric scattering transition.
- Cruise between planets and land on another body.
